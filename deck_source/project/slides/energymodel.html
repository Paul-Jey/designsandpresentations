Electrical Energy Model

P_shop(t) = Σ P_k(t) + P_comp + P_conv + Σ (P_light + P_fan)

E = Σ P(t)·Δt ; E_saved = E_base − E_ctrl

MD = max over 15 min of P̄ / PF (kVA)

Bill = E·c_E + max(MD, 0.9·CD)·c_D

IER = E_non-processing / E_total ; CO₂ = E_saved × EF

REFERENCE SHOP · INPUTS

10 CNC machines, 2 shifts, 300 days/yr

7.5 / 3.0 / 1.2 / 0.35 kW per machine state

22 kW compressor, 2.2 kW conveyor, 4 zones of lights and fans

₹7.50/kWh + ₹608/kVA/month (TNERC HT-I)

EF 0.710 kg CO₂/kWh (CEA v21)

Δt = 10 s, fixed seed, one assumptions file

MODELLED

9

Factory bills charge energy and demand, so the model tracks both kWh and kVA.

Stochastic shift model: random material waits, tool changes, breakdowns, short absences, tea breaks and lunch; 30 % of machines left on overnight in the baseline. Detector model keeps the Phase I 10 % miss rate and adds occlusion bursts. Every number on later slides comes from this code; none is typed by hand.
