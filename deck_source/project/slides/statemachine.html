Operator present means hands off: presence is a veto on every shutdown.

Powers are assumptions for a vertical machining centre, swept in the sensitivity study. Leaving deep standby is charged a 10-minute warm-up at 4 kW in the model, because precision machines need thermal stability (thermal effects cause up to 75 % of geometric errors, Fraunhofer IPK). That is why precision machines are advisory only.
