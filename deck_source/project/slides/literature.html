Literature Review and Research Gap

Machine-tool energy states

Gutowski 2006 · Kara and Li 2011 · Li, Zein et al. 2011

Fixed readiness load dominates when the machine is not cutting.

Switch-off policies

Mouzon et al. 2007 · Squeo et al. 2019

Standby saves energy if idle time beats break-even; decided by timers only.

Vision occupancy

Tien et al. 2021 · Kong et al. 2022 · Lin et al. 2023

Cameras detect people and equipment use; 17 to 24 % HVAC savings in buildings.

Current sensing (NILM)

Gebbe et al. 2019 · Suzuki et al. 2017

CT current tells machine state (85 % or better) but not who is there.
