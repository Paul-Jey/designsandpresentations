42 %

of India's electricity is used by industry (FY 2023-24, CEA General Review 2025)

14.8 %

of a machining line's energy reached actual cutting; the rest ran auxiliary and constant loads (Gutowski et al.)

~10 %

of a plant's electricity makes compressed air; leaks waste 20 to 30 % of it (US DOE)

Machines wait for material, operators take breaks, lines stop for breakdowns: coolant pumps, hydraulics, fans, lights and compressors keep running.
