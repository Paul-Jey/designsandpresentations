## 7.1 COST OF A DEPLOYMENT

The cost of equipping the reference shop is broken down in <<tab:cost>>. The edge computer itself is a small part of the total. ## 7.2 STANDARDS AND CONTROL PATHS

<<tab:std>> lists the standards and protocols that a real deployment would rely on, and the role each would play. They fall into two groups: those that let the node talk to equipment, and those that define how the savings should be managed and proven.

@tab std

On the equipment side, the simplest path is a relay or a Modbus input and output module, which works with any load that has a contactor or a remote stop input. This is enough for lighting, fans, the conveyor and the compressor. For machines, a direct connection is better. PROFIenergy allows a controller to tell a PROFINET machine to enter a pause mode that its builder has defined, so the machine itself decides which units to switch off and how to come back. OPC UA, and the machine tool companion specification published by umati, would let the node read the machine's own state, which could later replace or confirm the CT signal.

On the management side, the node fits naturally into an energy management system under ISO 50001. The idle-energy ratio defined in Chapter 5 is a suitable energy performance indicator in the sense of ISO 50006, because it is easy to measure, easy to explain to a shop owner and directly affected by the control.

## 7.3 MEASUREMENT AND VERIFICATION PLAN

The savings in this report are modelled. Before they can be claimed for a real shop, they must be measured. We propose a plan based on Option B of the International Performance Measurement and Verification Protocol (IPMVP), retrofit isolation with measurement of all parameters, which is consistent with ISO 50015.

- Install CT clamps on every machine feeder and on the feeders for the compressor, lighting and fans, logged at one-minute intervals.
- Run the node in ADVISORY mode with alerts hidden for two weeks. This records the baseline while also logging what the node would have done.
- Switch to AUTO mode for the whitelisted loads for a further two weeks.
- Normalise both periods by production, as kWh per part, and by operating hours, then compare. Report the idle-energy ratio before and after.

The baseline period also gives the two inputs that the sensitivity study identified as most important, the real idle power of each machine and the real frequency of idle periods, so the model can be recalibrated before the AUTO period begins.

## 7.4 DEPLOYMENT ROADMAP

We see deployment in five steps, each of which reduces risk before the next.

- Step 1, bench demonstration: a small fan or motor acts as the machine, with a current sensor and a relay, to show the complete chain from operator absence to switching. The parts cost about Rs 1,000 beyond the existing node, and the plan is given in Appendix 3.
- Step 2, one machine in a partner MSME in advisory mode, to measure detection accuracy under real conditions and the real idle power.
- Step 3, automatic control of shared loads, which carry no risk to product quality.
- Step 4, automatic standby for whitelisted machines, with operator feedback collected on nuisance events.
- Step 5, integration with the plant's systems through MQTT and, where available, PROFIenergy or OPC UA, with verified savings reported under ISO 50015.

## 7.5 BENCH DEMONSTRATION

For the final review, the full chain can be shown on the bench with the existing Jetson. A 12 V fan stands in for the machine, an INA219 sensor measures its current, and a relay switches it. A desk in front of the camera is defined as the operator region. When the operator sits at the desk with the fan running, the state is PROCESSING. When the fan is stopped but the operator stays, the state is IDLE-ATTENDED and nothing happens. When the operator walks away, a timer shortened to 30 s for the demonstration counts down, the relay opens and the dashboard shows the energy and rupees avoided rising. When the operator returns, the fan is restarted only by pressing a resume button, which shows that the node never starts a load on its own. Switching to ADVISORY mode and repeating the sequence shows an alert instead of an action.
